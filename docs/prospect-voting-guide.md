# Prospect Feedback & Voting

Each prospect has a forum thread where members share their experiences and cast a vote. Your input genuinely shapes recruitment decisions, so take a moment if you've interacted with the prospect.

## Leaving Feedback
- Post **your own** experiences with the prospect, in-game or in Discord. Don't just echo what others wrote.
- Keep it **factual** — short, specific, no speculation or back-and-forth debates.
- Negative feedback is welcome when it's honest. **No retaliation**, ever. If anyone gives you grief for your comment or vote, DM @Royal Secretary.
- A written reason is especially helpful on **No** votes — it lets the recruitment team address concerns early, or give the prospect something to work on if they're rejected.

## Voting
Three buttons sit on the vote embed in the prospect's forum thread:
- **Yes** — positive interactions, you'd be happy to have them.
- **No** — negative experience. A short reason form will open; please fill it in.
- **Unsure** — you haven't interacted enough to judge.

Changed your mind? **Change your vote** — don't add a second one. Duplicates get removed.

## When Does a Prospect Pass?
A vote passes when there are **at least 10 Yes votes** AND **at least 80% Yes ratio** (`yes / (yes + no)`). Unsure votes are recorded but don't affect the ratio.
