# Royal Battalion — Squad Server Rules & Admin Guidelines

---

## SECTION 1: GENERAL RULES

1. **Respect** — No bullying, discrimination, or derogatory language toward anyone.
2. **No spam** — No screaming, sound effects, or music in voice/text chat.
3. **Advertising**
   - 3.1 No advertising without staff permission.
   - 3.2 Streamers may promote only their own channel.
   - 3.3 Streamers must use in-game overlays or a 5-minute delay to protect the server and prevent stream sniping.
4. **Recruiting** — Only Royal Battalion may recruit on the server.
5. **Balancing** — Clans must help balance teams when asked by an Admin. All players must actively participate, even if a team-change request was denied.

---

## SECTION 2: GAME RULES

> By playing on this server you accept these rules. Ignorance does not grant immunity.

1. **Admin discretion** — All rules are enforced at Admin discretion. Do what Admins ask.
2. **No intentional teamkilling** — Includes revenge TKs and "self-defence" TKs.
3. **Fair play**
   - 3.1 No third-party software or game exploits.
   - 3.2 No ghosting (sharing info with the enemy team).
5. **Squads required** — All players must be in a squad; create one if none are available.
6. **Player names** — Must use Latin characters and be readable.
7. **No drama** — Stop arguments in chat if an Admin tells you to.

> Issues with Admin decisions can be reported via the **RB Support Bot**.

---

## SECTION 3: MAIN CAMPING RULES

1. **Main camping is prohibited** — Do not position assets, vehicles, infantry, or mines to target enemies moving in/out of their main.
   - **Rule of thumb:** Do not engage enemies between their main and their first capture point, or within 450 m of their main — whichever distance is greater.
   - 1.1 If objectives/FOBs are within the main-camp boundary, or enemies are fighting from that boundary, main-camp rules do not apply.
   - 1.2 Passing close to enemy main while flanking is allowed, as long as you do not engage.
   - 1.3 Admins have final word. Move if told to move.

---

## SECTION 4: SQUAD RULES

> **Mechanized infantry squads** may append "INF" to a valid vehicle claim name (e.g. "BTR-80 INF"). All other rules still apply, including Vehicle Rule 7.

1. Do not create a squad unless you intend to lead it.
2. Squad Leaders must use the Squad Leader kit at all times.
3. Squad Leaders must communicate in English in Local and Command chat.
4. Squads must play active objectives. If most other SLs or an Admin on duty say you aren't, move to the nearest active objective.
5. Squads may only lock at 3+ players (SL + 2). Unlock if an Admin requests it.
6. **Specialized squads** (vehicle, mortar, etc.) may lock with fewer than 3. Logistics squads are **not** specialized.

---

## SECTION 5: VEHICLE RULES

> **Priority:** Earliest squad created with a valid name gets the claim.
> **Exempt from vehicle rules:** Logistic vehicles, transport trucks, open-top MG cars.
> **Mobile Spawn Vehicle (MSV)** is claimable.

1. **Claims**
   - 1.1 Cannot claim more vehicles than exist on the layer.
   - 1.2 You claim only one vehicle. Track your respawn timer.
   - 1.3 You may use an unclaimed vehicle only if no other squad has claimed it.
2. **Crewman requirement** — Vehicles requiring the Crewman role must be 2-manned with Crewman kits at all times.
3. **Naming clarity** — Generic names (Armour, TANK, IFV, APC) are invalid if ambiguous. If multiple vehicles of the same type exist, spell out the specific name.
4. **Activity**
   - 4.1 AFK / unused / undermanned vehicles lose their claim.
   - 4.2 If the claiming squad is away from main, the claim isn't voided instantly — communicate.
5. **No heli ramming.**
6. **Heli squads** — Max 3 players.
7. **Vehicle squad size** — Limited to the vehicle's capacity. Scout/light vehicle squads (BRDM, RWS, Techies) may have up to 9.
8. **No console squad creation** — Offenders receive squad-creation time limits.

---

## SECTION 6: SEEDING RULES

1. Fight only for the middle cap(s).
2. No gun emplacements.
3. No sandbag towers.
4. Only unarmed trucks and light vehicles.
5. Do not camp enemy HABs/spawns.
6. Do not destroy enemy FOBs/radios.
7. **Skirmish layers:** HABs/FOBs must be 200 m+ from the fighting objective.
8. **Pre-made seeding layers:** Do not build FOBs or radios until the round goes live.

---

## SECTION 7: COMMANDER RULES

1. Commanders who troll their team with commander assets will be blocked from the role.
2. SLs who take Commander must use its abilities promptly; the Commander's squad must actively participate.
3. Being Commander may void your vehicle claim.

## SECTION 9: ADMIN GUIDELINES (INTERNAL)

### 9A — Tickets & Sharing Information with Players

- **Never share internal details** with players. This includes: evidence from the evidence vault, internal deliberations on punishments.
- If a player asks for evidence, respond: *"We do not share internal details, including evidence. You may report us to OWI, and we will let them review our evidence."*
- If a player reports someone, respond: *"Thanks, we'll review the evidence and handle the player appropriately."* — Do not specify the punishment, even if evidence is insufficient.
- **Ping @Whitelist Officer** in the evidence chat whenever you ban/warn a player from a whitelisted clan.

### 9B — Global Chat / Broadcasts

- Almost never use global chat broadcasts.
- **Only exception:** Calling for RB or external clan members to balance next round — and only after discussion in in-game Admin chat. When in doubt, ask first.

### 9C — Changing Layers / Ending Matches

- **Emergency use only** — meaning only when a layer is broken (e.g. vehicles not spawning).
- Process: Ping a second admin in 🔱︱server-admins → if both agree, ping an @Admin Officer for the final call.
- **Do NOT** skip layers because players say it's boring, or because "it fits better," or to test buttons.
- **Do NOT** change layers during weekend rotations.
- **Exception:** During seeding, press End Match when 65+ players are reached to transition to the next layer.

### 9D — Community Ban List (CBL)

- **Never delete expired bans** — they feed into CBL risk-rating calculations.
- **Do delete:** duplicate entries and bans that were issued and lifted the same calendar day (regardless of offence). Ping @Admin Officer for deletions.
- Review every CBL-related ticket carefully; the ban list may contain duplicates or same-day bans.

### 9E — Ticket Conduct Reminders (Admin Officer Notice)

1. **Neutral and respectful tone.** If you cannot remain neutral, leave the ticket and tag a senior admin or Admin Officer. Do not return insults or sass.
2. **Do not over-share.** You may tell a player *why* they were banned, but never show evidence or admin deliberations. Third parties cannot request info about another player's ban.
