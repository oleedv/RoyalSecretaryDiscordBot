ROYAL BATTALION - PROSPECT EVALUATION CRITERIA
================================================

This document defines the criteria used to evaluate prospect applications for Royal Battalion (RB), a gaming community for Squad.

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
------------------------
- Must be 18 years or older (confirmed via date of birth on application)
- Must NOT be in another clan that has a Squad server (dual-clanning not allowed)
- Steam ID must be valid and match a real account
- Must not have concerning active bans on Community Ban List (CBL)
- VAC bans older than 1500 days can be ignored; recent VAC bans require discussion and mentor discretion
- No inappropriate or offensive name history on BattleMetrics

PROSPECT PERIOD REQUIREMENTS
-----------------------------
- Prospect period: 4 weeks total
- Must accumulate 16 hours of in-game time during the 4 weeks
- Must be present on Discord while playing on the server (voice channel)
- Discord and in-game name must match
- Must uphold RB discord and server rules at all times
- Seeding time counts toward their hours; Discord VC is NOT required during seeding

VOTING REQUIREMENTS
-------------------
- Voting starts at the beginning of week 3
- Must wait the full 2-week voting period even if threshold is met early
- Need 80% positive feedback
- Minimum of 10 positive votes required
- If requirements not met: 3-week cooling off period before reapplying

MENTOR CHECKLIST (PRE-INTERVIEW)
---------------------------------
1. Verify the application form is fully filled out (all fields)
2. Confirm applicant is 18+ from date of birth
3. Confirm not dual-clanning (check previous clan field)
4. Steam ID correctly resolves to a BattleMetrics RCON profile
5. Cross-reference with Community Ban List (CBL) - check risk rating and active bans
6. Review BattleMetrics for inappropriate past names
7. Check for VAC bans (>1500 days old = acceptable, otherwise discuss)
8. Post "Checked" in ticket to signal all checks are green

RED FLAGS (concerns to highlight)
----------------------------------
- Any active bans on CBL (especially multiple or from well-known communities)
- High CBL risk rating (7+/10 is very concerning, 4-6 warrants attention)
- Active BattleMetrics bans on other servers
- Multiple expired bans across many different servers (pattern of behavior)
- VAC ban less than 1500 days old
- Very low hours in Squad (e.g., under 100 hours claimed but no BM history to support it)
- Already in another Squad clan (dual-clanning)
- Under 18 years old
- First/last seen data showing very recent first appearance (potential alt account)
- Very low game connections or average session time (suggesting casual play inconsistent with commitment)
- No Discord activity history (never messaged or joined voice)
- Applicant's stated hours don't match BattleMetrics recorded playtime

GREEN FLAGS (positives to highlight)
-------------------------------------
- Clean CBL record (0 risk rating, positive reputation points)
- No BattleMetrics bans at all
- Significant playtime on RB servers specifically
- Active seeding participation (shows community support)
- Already active on RB Discord (messages, voice time, reactions)
- Good average session length (shows commitment per play session)
- Recent and consistent play activity (not just a one-time visitor)
- Has competitive interest (aligns with RB's competitive scene)
- Previous clan experience without issues (shows familiarity with clan structure)

OVERALL ASSESSMENT GUIDANCE
----------------------------
- If ALL checks are green (no bans, good hours, 18+, not dual-clanning): straightforward approval for interview
- If minor concerns exist (e.g., a couple expired bans from years ago, slightly low hours): note them but likely fine, discuss during interview
- If moderate concerns exist (e.g., active ban on one server, recent VAC ban, dual-clanning with non-Squad clan): flag for mentor discussion, may need management input
- If major concerns exist (e.g., multiple active bans, high CBL risk, under 18, dual-clanning with Squad clan): likely denial, escalate to senior mentor

IMPORTANT NOTES
---------------
- This AI assessment is a tool to ASSIST mentors, not replace their judgment
- Mentors always have final say on prospect decisions
- Edge cases should be discussed with senior mentors or community officers
- The assessment should be factual and based on available data, not speculative
