# OWI Squad Code of Conduct
# Source: https://www.joinsquad.com/code-of-conduct
# Note: This content may become outdated. Check the source URL for the latest version.

---

## Welcome Squaddies

Players are encouraged to offer guidance to less experienced players. There is no wrong way to play the game -- there are only effective and ineffective tactics. The Steam forums and official channels serve as spaces for discussing strategies and providing development feedback.

## Inappropriate Content

Communications channels require mature conduct. The policy prohibits excessive profanity, obscenity, insults, personal attacks, accusations or abusive language. Circumventing language filters through asterisks or abbreviations remains prohibited.

Racist, misogynistic, sexist and ableist language is strictly forbidden. Harassment, abuse and bullying will not be tolerated.

## Constructive Behaviour

Feedback on game performance is welcomed but must remain constructive. "This sucks" tells OWI nothing about the problem or what would remedy it.

## No Flaming

Personal attacks and targeted harassment are prohibited. "But he flamed me first!" is not an acceptable reason to insult another player.

## Stay On Topic

Posts should remain relevant to designated forum sections; off-topic discussions belong in appropriate channels.

## No Spamming

Duplicate posting and chat flooding are prohibited.

## No Thread Bumping

Posting irrelevant content to keep threads visible is forbidden.

## Flagging Or Reporting Posts

Users should report problematic content using the flag system rather than public complaints.

## Reporting In-game Abuse

Players should report hostile behavior to the community team with server details and evidence. "Naming and shaming" users on forums is prohibited.

## Internet Savvy

Offworld Industries employees never request passwords. Personal details should not be shared with other players.

## Disciplinary Procedures

Rule violations result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal. Appeals can be submitted to support@offworldindustries.com.

The foundational principle: treat others as you wish to be treated.
